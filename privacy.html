Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This policy explains how the HoopTrax mobile app and this website handle your information. It is written to be clear and honest about our data practices; it is not legal advice. Please review it against your own circumstances before publishing.

HoopTrax ("we", "us") is a basketball game-tracking app for coaches. This policy describes what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

HoopTrax was previously called HoopScout. Only the name changed — the app, the operator and the data practices described below are the same.

Data controller: HoopTrax, operated by Ewout Samyn.
Contact: bballsidelineiq@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account you sign in with an email and password, or with Google or Apple Sign-In. We receive your email address and a unique account identifier so we can secure your account and sync your data across devices. Authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase, and by Google or Apple when you choose those sign-in options.

Content you create in the app

HoopTrax stores the coaching data you enter so it can be there when you reopen the app: team and season names, player names, jersey numbers and positions, and the game events and statistics you record. This data is saved on your device and synced to our backend so you don't lose it. See "Data you enter about players" below.

Advertising & device data

HoopTrax is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show ads and limit fraud, Google may process device and usage information such as a device advertising identifier, IP address, general location (derived from IP), device type and operating system, and interactions with ads. See section 4.

Diagnostic & usage data

We and our providers may collect limited technical information (such as crash reports and basic performance data) to keep the app working reliably. We do not use this to identify you personally.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the app — create your account, store and sync your teams, games and stats (legal basis: performance of a contract).
  • To keep HoopTrax free — show advertising through Google AdMob (legal basis: your consent for personalised ads where required, otherwise our legitimate interest in funding the app with non-personalised ads).
  • To secure and improve the service — prevent abuse, fix crashes, understand reliability (legal basis: legitimate interests).
  • To comply with the law — where we are legally required to.

3. Advertising and your consent

Advertising in HoopTrax is provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised (based on an advertising identifier) or non-personalised.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we show a Google-certified consent request (Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time it's required, and you can choose whether to allow personalised ads.
  • On iOS you will also see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; ads are non-personalised unless you allow tracking.
  • You can change or withdraw your ad consent at any time from within the app, and reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy here: policies.google.com/privacy.

A future paid version of HoopTrax may remove ads. If you upgrade, ad-related data collection stops for you.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers we need to run HoopTrax:

  • Google — AdMob advertising, and Google Sign-In if you use it (privacy policy).
  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting for your account and coaching data (privacy policy).
  • Apple — Apple Sign-In if you use it (privacy policy).

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data on servers outside your country, including outside the EEA. Where that happens, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and coaching data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or ask us to erase your data, we remove it from our systems within a reasonable period, except where we must keep certain records to comply with the law. Advertising identifiers are retained by Google according to Google's own policies.

7. Security

Access to your data is protected by authentication and per-account access rules, and data is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • erase your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent (for example, for personalised ads) at any time; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email bballsidelineiq@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law.

9. Data you enter about players

HoopTrax lets coaches record information about their players, such as names, jersey numbers and performance statistics. As the coach entering this data, you are responsible for having an appropriate basis to record it, and for informing players (or their parents/guardians where players are minors). We process this data only on your behalf to provide the app.

10. Children's privacy

HoopTrax is intended for coaches and adult users, not for use by children to create their own accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as HoopTrax evolves. When we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
